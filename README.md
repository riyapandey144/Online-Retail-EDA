# 🛍️ Online Retail II — Exploratory Data Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the Online Retail II dataset to identify important patterns in **sales, customers, products, and revenue**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- 🧹 Clean and prepare transaction data
- 📊 Analyze sales and revenue patterns
- 👥 Understand customer behaviour
- 🛒 Identify top-performing products
- 🌍 Analyze country-wise revenue
- 📅 Study monthly sales trends
- 🧪 Perform statistical hypothesis testing

---

## 📊 Key Findings

- 💵 Revenue is highly **right-skewed**, with a few very large transactions.
- 📦 **Quantity and Revenue** have a strong positive correlation (**0.8046**).
- 💰 **Price and Quantity** have almost no practical linear relationship (**-0.0038**).
- 👥 **Repeat customers** generate significantly more revenue than one-time customers.
- 🌍 The **United Kingdom** is the leading country in the top-country revenue analysis.
- 🛒 **REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER** is the highest-revenue product.
- 📅 Monthly analysis reveals useful revenue trends and fluctuations.

---

## 🧪 Statistical Testing

Three hypotheses were tested using a **5% significance level**.

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| 📦 Quantity vs Revenue | ✅ Significant |
| 💰 Price vs Quantity | ✅ Statistically significant but very weak |
| 👥 Repeat vs One-Time Customers | ✅ Significant |
